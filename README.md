## ◆ About

I'm a **Data Scientist & Engineer** with full-stack roots. My work lives at the seam between **data engineering, machine learning, and backend architecture** — designing the pipelines, services, and intelligence layers behind **ERP / POS platforms for SMEs across 7 African markets**.

I work the whole lifecycle: ingest messy real-world data → model it into clean, canonical schemas → wrap it in microservices → expose it through matching, scoring, and BI layers people actually use. **I'm data-driven for business intelligence — powering intelligence out of raw data is the whole point, and the real perk of data science.** If it touches data, it gets me there: building and maintaining trackers, pipelines, and the dashboards that sit on top of them. And I still ship full applications end-to-end, frontend included — several are live in production today.

```text
focus  ▸ product-data standardization APIs · matching & scoring engines
       ▸ data pipelines · trackers built & maintained · ETL & data quality
       ▸ master data & taxonomy modelling · multi-market compliance logic
       ▸ ML model development · BI dashboards (Power BI / Tableau)
```
